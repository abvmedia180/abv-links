First things first

So, what is Claude?

Claude is an AI you talk to, like texting a very smart, very patient friend. You don’t need to know anything technical. You just describe what you want in normal English, and Claude does the hard part.

For a website, that means you say something like:

You type: “Make me a clean, modern website for my design business. A home page, a portfolio, and a contact page. Warm colors. Here’s my bio and my photos.”

…and Claude writes the entire website, the words, the layout, the code, all of it. If you want something changed, you just say so: “make the header bigger,” “use a softer blue,” “add a page about my prints.” It changes it. No code, no menus, no stress.

That is exactly how your Blue Horse website was built. It was all just describing what we wanted, in plain words.

The recipe

How it’s done, step by step.

Six steps. Take them one at a time. Most people do the whole thing in an afternoon, and you only ever set it up once.

1

Describe your website to Claude

Go to claude.ai, sign up (it’s free to start), and tell it what you want, your pages, your info, the style, your photos. It writes the whole website for you. Ask for any changes until you love it.

Free to start~an hour of chatting
2

Make a free GitHub account

Go to github.com and sign up. This is the free “home” where your website will live. Signing up is exactly like making any other account, an email and a password.

Free~5 minutes
3

Put your files on GitHub and turn on “Pages”

You upload the website files Claude made into a GitHub “repository” (just a folder), then flip on a free setting called GitHub Pages. The moment you do, your site is live on the internet at a free address. Claude can walk you through every click.

Free hosting, forever~15 minutes
4

Buy your web address on Namecheap

Go to namecheap.com, search for the name you want (like yourname.com), and buy it. This is the one part that costs a little, about $10–15 for the whole year.

≈ $12 / year~5 minutes
5

Point your address to your website

This just means telling Namecheap and GitHub to shake hands, so your new address shows your website. You copy a few settings from GitHub into Namecheap. It sounds technical, but it’s literally copy-and-paste, and the video at the bottom shows every click. Claude can guide you too.

Free~10 minutes
6

You’re live! 🎉

That’s it. Your website is on the internet, at your own address, and you own all of it. Want to change something later? Just ask Claude and re-upload. No monthly website bills, no designer on retainer.
