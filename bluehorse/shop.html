Blue Horse Studio

Every brushstroke gives a horse another day.

Founder Melissa Auberty is a professional painter, and her celebrated Blue Horse Series is where the sanctuary’s name comes from. She gives 100% of every sale straight to the animals. When you buy a piece, you’re buying hay, farrier visits, and vet care.
