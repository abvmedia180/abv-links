The Short Answer

How much will I pay?

With a commercial Aetna or Empire BCBS plan, you pay your plan's copay, coinsurance or deductible rate. Without a qualifying plan, self-pay is $300 per visit. The 10-minute consult call is always free.

Not on the List?

What if my plan isn't accepted?

We do not accept UnitedHealthcare, Cigna, Oxford, EmblemHealth (GHI/HIP), Healthfirst, Fidelis, MetroPlus, Humana or other plans not listed above. Self-pay at $300 per visit is always available. Your plan may offer out-of-network benefits, so it is worth asking them.

Other visit fees: appointments canceled with less than 24 hours' notice, or missed entirely, incur a $200 no-show fee.

Insurance questions.

Do you take my insurance? +

We are in-network with commercial Aetna and Empire BCBS plans. We do not accept Medicare, Medicaid or HMO plans, or plans linked to them. Self-pay is $300 per visit. We talk through insurance and cost on the free consult call.

What does a visit cost? +

With a commercial Aetna or Empire BCBS plan, you pay your plan's copay, coinsurance or deductible rate. Self-pay is $300 per visit.

Is everything really by video? +

Yes. We are 100% telehealth, with no office to visit. Visits happen on a secure video platform, and the free consult is a phone call. You need to be physically in New York State at the time of each visit.

How long are visits? +

The consult call is about 10 minutes by phone. Your first full visit, the intake, is about 60 minutes by video. Follow-up visits are about 30 minutes.

Can I be seen if I am outside New York? +

Our clinicians are licensed in New York, so you need to be located in New York State during each visit. If you travel, schedule around the days you are in the state.
