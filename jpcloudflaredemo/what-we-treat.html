Medication Management

How does medication management work here?

Medication is never automatic. After a full evaluation, your clinician talks through your options, including whether medication makes sense for you at all. Medication is not prescribed at the intake visit. If it is clinically appropriate, it may be started at your first follow-up.

When a medication is started, the first follow-up is within about a week, with regular visits until things are stable. After that, follow-ups are spaced out based on what you need. Every prescription follows New York State rules and evidence-based guidelines.
