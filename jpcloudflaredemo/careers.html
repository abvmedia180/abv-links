Careers

Join a psychiatrist-ledtelehealth team.

We hire board-certified psychiatric nurse practitioners (PMHNP-BC) licensed in New York to provide evaluation and medication management for adults by video, as part of a team led by our medical director, Dr. Amanda Joseph.

Email your CV

Why Join Us

Why nurse practitioners join us

New graduates welcome. If you're coachable and care about doing this well, we'll teach you the rest.

  • Real training. Structured courses, protocols and case review led by our medical director, Dr. Amanda Joseph.
  • Start part-time. Keep your current nursing job while you build your caseload, with a path to full-time.
  • Competitive pay.
  • Benefits for full-time team members.
  • Support, not sink-or-swim. Clear protocols, a team to ask, and resources for every visit.

Still finishing school, or waiting on your NP license or DEA registration? Reach out anyway. We'll plan your start around it.

We invest in the people we hire, so they can feel confident from day one.

The Role

What does the work look like?

  • Adult outpatient psychiatry focused on ADHD, anxiety and depression
  • 100% telehealth, weekday schedule between 10 AM and 7 PM ET
  • Clinical approach designed by Dr. Joseph, with a team you can bring questions to

What we look for

  • PMHNP-BC board certification
  • Active, unrestricted New York State NP license and prescriptive authority
  • Warm, plain communication with high-achieving, busy adults

Send your CV and a short note to info@josephpsych.com with "PMHNP role" in the subject line.

Please do not send patient information.

Psychiatrist-led care for adults across New York. 100% telehealth.
