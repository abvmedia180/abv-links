Careers

Join a psychiatrist-ledtelehealth team.

We hire board-certified psychiatric nurse practitioners (PMHNP-BC) licensed in New York to provide evaluation and medication management for adults by video, as part of a team led by Dr. Amanda Joseph, MD.

Email your CV

The Role

What does the work look like?

  • Adult outpatient psychiatry focused on ADHD, anxiety and depression
  • 100% telehealth, weekday schedule between 10 AM and 7 PM ET
  • Continuity of care: you stay the clinician for your patients
  • Clinical approach designed by Dr. Joseph, with a team you can bring questions to

What we look for

  • PMHNP-BC board certification
  • Active, unrestricted New York State NP license and prescriptive authority
  • Warm, plain communication with high-achieving, busy adults

Send your CV and a short note to info@josephpsych.com with "PMHNP role" in the subject line. Please do not send patient information.

Psychiatrist-led ADHD, anxiety, and depression care. Video appointments weekdays 10 AM to 7 PM ET, serving all of New York State.
