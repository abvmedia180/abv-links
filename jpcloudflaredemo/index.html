Sound familiar?

You hold it all together on the outside. But inside, it's exhausting.

If any of these resonate, you're not alone, and you're in the right place.

"I have 47 tabs open in my brain at all times"

You're constantly juggling thoughts, forgetting things you just said you'd do, and feeling like you can't turn your brain off, even when you're exhausted.

"The anxiety never really goes away"

There's a constant low-level hum of worry. Am I doing enough? Did I forget something? What if they figure out I'm not as capable as they think?

"Some days I just can't get started"

The motivation isn't there. You know what you need to do but you physically cannot start. And then the guilt spiral begins.

"I can't focus no matter how hard I try"

You read the same paragraph five times. You sit down to study or work and two hours later you've done everything except the thing you needed to do.

"Everyone thinks I have it together"

You're excelling at work or school, but no one sees the hours of anxiety, the last-minute scrambles, or how hard you work just to keep up.

"I make lists for my lists"

Planners, apps, sticky notes. You've tried every system. They work for a week and then you're back to feeling scattered and behind.

It doesn't have to feel like this.

The racing, the masking, the exhaustion. With the right care, it settles.

From a racing mind to a steady one. That's the work we do.

Still Have Questions?

We've got answers.

Who will I see? +

You'll see one of the providers on our care team, all led by our medical director, Dr. Amanda Joseph, a board-certified psychiatrist.

You'll see your provider's name when you book.

When are appointments? +

Weekdays, 10 AM to 7 PM Eastern, by video. That includes after-work slots until 7 PM.

We do not offer weekend appointments.

How often will I be seen? +
  1. Week 1: Intake
  2. Week 2: Follow-up
  3. Week 3: Follow-up
  4. Week 4: Follow-up
  5. After that: At least once a month
Do you take my insurance? +

We are in-network with most commercial Aetna and Anthem Blue Cross Blue Shield (formerly Empire) plans.

We don't accept Medicare, Medicaid, HMO plans or plans linked to Medicare or Medicaid.

Self-pay is $300 per visit if you prefer. We'll confirm your insurance information before your first appointment.

Is everything really by video? +

Yes. We are 100% telehealth, with no office to visit. The free consult is a phone call.

You need to be in New York State at the time of each visit.

Can you prescribe ADHD medication? +

Yes, if it is clinically appropriate after a full evaluation. We follow New York State rules and evidence-based guidelines.

Medication is not prescribed at the intake. If it is right for you, it may start at your first follow-up.

Is medication required? +

No. Your clinician talks through your options with you.

Your plan is built around your symptoms and your preferences.

Do you offer therapy? +

We focus on psychiatric evaluation and medication management.

If you have a therapist, we are glad to coordinate with them, with your permission.
