Before you book

Who we see

  • Adults 18 and older who will be in New York State during visits.

  • All care is by video, weekdays 10 AM to 7 PM ET. The consult itself is a phone call.

Insurance and cost

  • We accept commercial Aetna and Empire BCBS plans.

  • No qualifying plan? Self-pay is $300 per visit.

  • We do not accept Medicare, Medicaid and HMO plans, including Medicare Advantage and managed Medicaid.

  • We do not accept UnitedHealthcare, Cigna, Oxford, EmblemHealth (GHI/HIP), Healthfirst, Fidelis, MetroPlus, Humana or any other plan.

Paperwork

  • We don't complete disability, FMLA, or school, work or housing accommodation paperwork, or ESA letters. Your records are always available to you on request.
