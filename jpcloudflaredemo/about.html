Who We Are

Who is Dr. Joseph?

Joseph Psychiatry was founded by Dr. Amanda Joseph, a board-certified psychiatrist who holds both an MD and an MBA. She served as Chief Resident in Psychiatry during her training and has 7+ years of experience in emergency and outpatient psychiatry. That work shaped how she meets patients: practical, supportive and without rush or assumptions.

Dr. Joseph never planned to open a private practice. She expected to keep working entirely within hospital systems until several senior mentors encouraged her to start a practice of her own. They told her she had a natural ability to connect with patients and a gift for understanding their needs. Their encouragement is a big part of why the practice exists, and it still guides the standard of care here.

"I started this practice because I wanted to give patients the kind of care I'd want for myself: someone actually taking the time to understand what's going on, not just treating what's on the surface."

Dr. Amanda Joseph, MD · Founder & Medical Director

Dr. Joseph serves her local church community and sits on the board of a nonprofit organization that provides essential resources and medical care for underserved people in the United States and Africa. Joseph Psychiatry supports that work through charitable contributions.

Outside of work, she loves traveling with her husband and two children. Their trips usually include zoos, aquariums or anything with wildlife. She also loves cooking, often with recipes inspired by her Indian heritage. Her family's favorite is her homemade mango pie.

Our Approach

How do we approach care?

Simple, practical and patient-centered. Every person has a different story, and we make it a priority to understand yours. We focus on what you are dealing with right now and what could help you feel and function better in daily life.

All appointments are secure video visits, so you can be seen from wherever you feel most at ease. The process is straightforward: a brief consult call, a full intake, then regular follow-ups to check in and adjust.
