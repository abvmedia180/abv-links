How it works

Understanding the process,every step of the way.

Care starts with a free 10-minute phone call, then a 60-minute video intake and regular 30-minute follow-ups, all with the same clinician. Visits are by secure video, weekdays 10 AM to 7 PM ET, while you are located in New York State.

Book your free 10-minute call

The Short Answer

What happens after I book?

Your free consult is a quick 10-minute phone call at the time you booked. If we are a good fit, you set up your patient account, complete your forms and book your intake. From there, follow-ups are scheduled around your needs.

Your First Steps

Four steps, no surprises.

1

Step 1: Free 10-minute consult call

About 10 minutesBy phone

Your free consult is a quick 10-minute phone call, not a visit. We'll see if we're the right fit and answer your questions. Please review our patient policies before the call; a copy is available on request.

2

Step 2: Account setup and forms

Before your intake

After your consult call, you will get an email to create your patient account, complete intake forms and consents, submit insurance details and add a payment method. Forms must be finished before your intake or the visit may need to be rescheduled.

3

Step 3: Intake appointment

About 60 minutesBy video

Your first full visit focuses on your history, symptoms and goals so your clinician can build a personalized plan. Medication is not prescribed at the intake. If appropriate, it may be started at your first follow-up.

4

Step 4: Follow-up visits

About 30 minutesBy video

Follow-ups review progress and adjust the plan. If medication is started, the first follow-up is within one week and visits stay weekly until your dose is stable, then they are spaced out based on your needs.

Good to Know

How do telehealth visits work?

All visits take place on a secure, HIPAA-compliant video platform. Your appointment link is sent by email and text. Our clinicians are licensed in New York and can only treat patients who are physically in New York State at the time of each visit.

What should I prepare before my intake?

  • A list of your current medications
  • Your past mental health history
  • Any relevant labs or previous evaluations
  • The questions or concerns you want to discuss

Policies

What should I know about attendance and services?

Attendance

Please join on time. There is a 10-minute grace period. Appointments canceled with less than 24 hours' notice, or missed entirely, incur a $200 no-show fee.

What we don't provide

To keep care safe and appropriate, we do not provide:

  • Emergency or crisis services
  • Court-ordered evaluations
  • Disability paperwork
  • FMLA paperwork
  • Accommodation paperwork (school, work, housing and similar)
  • ESA (emotional support animal) letters
  • Forensic evaluations
  • Letters for legal cases
  • Chronic benzodiazepine prescribing

If you are having an emergency, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency room. In crisis, call or text 988.

Quick Answers

Common questions.

How often will I be seen? +

Your first month: an intake visit plus three weekly follow-ups (4 visits). After that, at least one visit a month.

Do you take my insurance? +

We are in-network with most commercial Aetna and Anthem Blue Cross Blue Shield (formerly Empire) plans. We don't accept Medicare or Medicaid. We also don't accept HMO plans or plans linked to Medicare or Medicaid. Self-pay is $300 per visit. We'll confirm your insurance information before your first appointment.

Can you prescribe ADHD medication? +

If medication is clinically appropriate after a full evaluation, yes, following New York State rules and evidence-based guidelines. Medication is not prescribed at the intake visit. If it is appropriate, it may be started at your first follow-up.

Is there anything you do not provide? +

We do not provide emergency or crisis services, court-ordered or forensic evaluations, disability or FMLA paperwork, school, work or housing accommodation paperwork, ESA letters, or letters for legal cases. The full list is on our What to expect page.
