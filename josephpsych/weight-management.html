Home / Weight Management

Your mind and your metabolism.
One provider.

Medication-assisted weight management, including FDA-approved GLP-1 medications, built into your psychiatric care. Whole-person treatment from a team that already understands the mental health side of weight.

Why We Offer This
Weight care belongs in a psychiatry practice.

Many psychiatric medications affect weight, and weight and mood are deeply connected. Instead of sending you to a separate clinic, the same provider who manages your mental health can evaluate and manage weight-loss medication in the same visit, on the same platform. No second office, no coordination burden, no repeating your story.

Already a patient here?

Weight management is an included part of your comprehensive care. There is no separate program and no extra program fee. If weight, or weight gain from a medication, is on your mind, bring it up at any regular appointment and we will take it from there.

Here just for weight management?

Welcome. You will get a full medical evaluation, not just a prescription. Family members of current patients are especially welcome. It starts with the same free 10-minute consult call as everything else we do.

How It Works
Careful, medical, and unhurried.
1

Comprehensive evaluation

Your health history, weight history, current medications, and screening for eating patterns and mood conditions. We look at the whole picture before anything is prescribed.

2

Baseline lab work

Simple lab work at a Quest or LabCorp location near you, so treatment decisions are grounded in your actual numbers.

3

Medication, if it's right for you

If medication is clinically appropriate, we start it and adjust the dose carefully. The evaluation decides, which means a prescription is never guaranteed. That's what makes this medicine rather than a vending machine.

4

Ongoing follow-up

Monthly telehealth visits while your dose is being adjusted, then less frequent maintenance visits, with weight tracking between appointments. This is continuous medical care, not a one-time script.

FDA-approved medications only. Always.

We do not prescribe compounded or gray-market GLP-1 copies, ever. Only FDA-approved medications from licensed pharmacies and manufacturers. And we help every patient find their most affordable legitimate option: insurance prior-authorization support, manufacturer direct-purchase programs, and the FDA-approved oral option. Coverage varies by plan, so we never promise it. We navigate it with you.

Straight Answers
What people ask us.

Will I definitely get a prescription?

→No. The evaluation determines whether medication is appropriate and safe for you. If it isn't the right tool, we will tell you honestly and talk through what is.

Do you prescribe compounded semaglutide or tirzepatide?

→No. FDA-approved medications from licensed pharmacies only. If a price online looks too good to be true, it usually is.

Is this covered by insurance?

→Visits are billed through your insurance like any other appointment (we're in-network with Aetna and Blue Cross Blue Shield), or private pay at a flat $300 per session. Medication coverage varies by plan, and we help you navigate prior authorization and manufacturer programs.

Can my spouse or family member come just for this?

→Yes. Family members of current patients are welcome as weight-management patients. They start with the same free consult call.

Ready to talk about it?

A free 10-minute call to see whether this is the right fit. No pressure, no commitment.
