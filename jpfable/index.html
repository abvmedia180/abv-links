Our Specialty

ADHD in Adults

ADHD doesn't always look like what you'd expect — especially when you're high-functioning. It looks like chronic disorganization, emotional overwhelm, an inability to "just do the thing," and years of being told you're "smart but not reaching your potential." Many of our patients weren't diagnosed until their 20s or 30s. ADHD is our primary focus, and we understand the nuances that generalist providers often miss — especially in adults who've learned to mask their symptoms.

Difficulty focusing despite being capable Emotional dysregulation & rejection sensitivity Executive function challenges Late diagnosis in adulthood Evidence-based medication management

Anxiety

That constant inner critic telling you everything will go wrong. We help quiet the noise with evidence-based medication management — and untangle the anxiety that's so often intertwined with ADHD.

Generalized anxiety Social anxiety Panic attacks Performance anxiety Imposter syndrome

Depression

Sometimes it's not sadness — it's numbness, exhaustion, or just going through the motions. We meet you wherever you are, and distinguish depression from burnout, grief, or ADHD.

Persistent low mood Loss of motivation Burnout & emotional fatigue Difficulty finding joy Medication & ongoing support

Weight Management

Weight and mental health are deeply connected, and many psychiatric medications affect weight. We manage both together, in the same visit, with the same provider.

Dr. Amanda Joseph, MD

Founder & Medical Director · Board-certified psychiatrist · 7+ years clinical experience

Dr. Joseph is a board-certified psychiatrist with over seven years of clinical experience spanning hospital psychiatry, outpatient clinics, and private practice, including serving as chief resident at Jamaica Hospital Medical Center, one of the highest-volume psychiatric programs in New York. After years in institutional settings, she kept seeing the same problem: patients moving through crowded, impersonal systems where no one had time to learn their story.

She founded Joseph Psychiatry to do things differently. Here, every patient gets the time they deserve — thorough evaluations, individualized treatment plans, and a provider who actually listens. Her specialty is working with high-functioning adults dealing with ADHD, anxiety, and depression, often for the first time and often after years of being told "you're fine."

Still Have Questions?

We've got answers.

How soon can I get an appointment? +

Usually within 1–2 weeks of your consult call. We don't do long waitlists — when you're ready, we move quickly.

What insurance do you accept, and what does it cost? +

We accept Aetna and Blue Cross Blue Shield (Anthem / Empire). We verify your benefits for free during the consult call so you know your copay upfront. Private pay is a flat $300 per session. We do not accept Medicare, Medicaid, or HMO plans.

Can you prescribe controlled substances (Adderall, etc.)? +

Yes. If ADHD medication is clinically appropriate after a thorough evaluation, our providers can prescribe stimulant and non-stimulant medications. We follow evidence-based prescribing guidelines.

I'm nervous about taking medication. Is it required? +

Medication is never forced or pressured. We'll discuss all your options, explain benefits and risks, and respect your decision. Many patients want to start with understanding their diagnosis first.

Do you provide therapy/talk therapy? +

We focus on psychiatric evaluation and medication management. If you'd benefit from therapy alongside medication, we can coordinate with your therapist or help connect you with one.

Can I see you if I live outside New York? +

Currently we're licensed to provide telehealth services to patients located in New York State. If you're in NY — whether that's Manhattan, Long Island, or upstate — we can see you.
